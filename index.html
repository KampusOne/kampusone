OUR STORY

It started with a simple frustration.

Why should a student need five group chats, three websites and word of mouth just to navigate one school day?

KampusOne began as a student-built answer to the everyday confusion around university life: missing information, forgotten deadlines, hard-to-find locations, scattered materials and campus services that are difficult to trust.

What started as an idea at the University of Benin grew into a bigger mission — to create a dependable digital layer for university communities across Africa.
